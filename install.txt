========================================================
Y.D.R.B.N. - QUICK INSTALLATION GUIDE (iOS & ANDROID)
Live App: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
========================================================

Y.D.R.B.N. is a 100% offline-capable Progressive Web App (PWA).
You can install it directly to your phone's home screen without
needing the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.


--------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: APPLE / iPHONE & iPAD (SAFARI)
--------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: OPEN IN SAFARI
- Open the Safari browser app on your iPhone or iPad.
  (Note: Use Safari, not Chrome, as Apple requires Safari
  to add apps to your home screen).
- Go to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/

STEP 2: OPEN THE SHARE MENU
- Tap the "Share" button at the bottom of Safari
  (the square icon with an arrow pointing straight up).

STEP 3: ADD TO HOME SCREEN
- Scroll down the Share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen".
- Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
- Y.D.R.B.N. is now installed on your home screen!


--------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: ANDROID (CHROME)
--------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: OPEN IN CHROME
- Open the Chrome browser app on your Android phone.
- Go to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/

STEP 2: INSTALL THE APP (CHOOSE EITHER OPTION)
- OPTION A (In-App Button):
  Tap the pulsing "INSTALL" button in the top bar of the app
  (or tap the Gear icon in the bottom-right corner and tap
  "Install App"), then confirm.

- OPTION B (Chrome Menu - Always Available):
  Tap Chrome's three-dot menu (top-right corner) and tap
  "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
