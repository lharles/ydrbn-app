Locale: EN | App: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
========================================================

--------------------------------------------------------
APPLE / iPHONE (SAFARI)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Safari to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
2. Tap the Share button (square with upward arrow) in bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and select 'Add to Home Screen'.

--------------------------------------------------------
ANDROID (CHROME)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Chrome to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
2. Tap the pulsing 'Install' icon in top receiver bar.
3. Or tap three dots (⋮) in top-right and tap 'Install app'.
