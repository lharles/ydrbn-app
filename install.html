INSTALL Y.D.R.B.N.

100% Offline App • No App Store Required

Open Y.D.R.B.N. App →
🍎 iPhone / iOS 🤖 Android

🍎 Apple / iPhone & iPad (Safari)

STEP 1: Open in Safari
  • Open the Safari app (use Safari, not Chrome) and visit:
    lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
STEP 2: Tap the Share Button
  • Tap the Share icon at the bottom center of Safari (the square with an upward arrow).
STEP 3: Add to Home Screen
  • Scroll down the menu and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Tap Add in the top-right corner. The app icon will appear right on your home screen!

🤖 Android (Chrome)

STEP 1: Open in Chrome
  • Open Chrome and visit:
    lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
STEP 2: Install to Home Screen (Two Easy Ways)
  • Option A (In-App Button): Tap the pulsing INSTALL button in the top bar of the app (or open the bottom-right Gear settings menu and tap Install App).
  • Option B (Chrome Menu — Always Works): Tap Chrome's three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner and select Install app (or Add to Home screen).

💻 Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  • Open lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/ and click the Install icon inside the right side of your browser's address bar.
