========================================================
Y.D.R.B.N. - FULL APP RESET & UPDATE GUIDE
Live App: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
========================================================

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: APPLE / iPHONE (iOS) FULL RESET STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: DELETE THE OLD HOME SCREEN ICON
- Find the Y.D.R.B.N. icon on your iPhone home screen.
- Press and hold the icon until the menu pops up.
- Tap "Delete Bookmark" (or "Remove App" -> "Delete").
- (Required so your iPhone drops the old logo icon).

STEP 2: CLOSE OLD SAFARI TABS
- Open the Safari browser app.
- Tap the Tabs icon (two overlapping squares in bottom-right).
- Close any open tabs showing Y.D.R.B.N. or github.io.

STEP 3: CLEAR SAFARI WEBSITE DATA FOR GITHUB.IO
- Open your iPhone's main "Settings" app (silver gear icon).
- Open Safari settings:
  * iOS 18 or newer: Scroll to the bottom, tap "Apps", then "Safari".
  * iOS 17 or older: Scroll down the main list and tap "Safari".
- Scroll to the very bottom and tap "Advanced" -> "Website Data".
- Type "github.io" in the search bar at the top.
- Swipe left on "github.io" (or tap Edit) and tap "Delete".

STEP 4: OPEN FRESH IN SAFARI
- Open the Safari app (use Safari, not Chrome).
- Go to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
- Tap the circular Refresh arrow in the address bar once.

STEP 5: ADD BACK TO HOME SCREEN
- Tap the "Share" button at the bottom middle of Safari.
- Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
- Confirm you see the new plain album cover icon, then tap "Add".


--------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: ANDROID FULL RESET STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: UNINSTALL THE OLD APP
- Find the Y.D.R.B.N. icon on your home screen or app drawer.
- Press and hold the icon.
- Tap "App info" (the "i" circle) and tap "Uninstall" (or drag straight up to the "Uninstall" trash can at the top).
- NOTE: Do NOT tap "Remove" (that only deletes the shortcut).

STEP 2: CLEAR OLD SITE STORAGE IN CHROME
- Open the Chrome browser app.
- Go to: https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
- Tap the "Tune / Sliders" icon on the left side of the address bar.
- Tap "Cookies and site data" (or "Site settings").
- Tap the Trash Can icon (or "Clear & reset" / "Delete data") and confirm.

STEP 3: HARD REFRESH THE PAGE
- Back on the Y.D.R.B.N. page in Chrome, tap the three dots (top right).
- Tap the Refresh arrow at the top of the menu.

STEP 4: REINSTALL WITH THE NEW ICON
- Tap the "INSTALL" button in the top bar (or tap the Gear icon in the bottom right and tap "Install App").
- Or tap Chrome's three dots (top right) and select "Install app".
