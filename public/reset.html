🍎 Apple / iPhone (iOS) Full Reset

STEP 1: Delete the Old Home Screen Icon
  • Find the Y.D.R.B.N. icon on your iPhone home screen.
  • Press and hold the icon until the menu pops up.
  • Tap Delete Bookmark (or Remove App → Delete).
  • Required so your iPhone drops the old logo icon.
STEP 2: Close Old Safari Tabs
  • Open Safari, tap the Tabs button (bottom-right), and close any open Y.D.R.B.N. or github.io tabs.
STEP 3: Clear Safari Website Data
  • Open your iPhone's main Settings app (silver gear).
  • iOS 18+: Scroll to the bottom → tap Apps → tap Safari.
    iOS 17 & older: Scroll down and tap Safari.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Advanced → Website Data.
  • Search for github.io at the top, swipe left on it, and tap Delete.
STEP 4: Open Fresh & Re-Add to Home Screen
  • Open Safari and go to lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
  • Tap the circular Refresh arrow in the address bar.
  • Tap the Share button at the bottom → Add to Home Screen → Add.

🤖 Android Full Reset

STEP 1: Uninstall the Old App
  • Press and hold the Y.D.R.B.N. icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  • Tap App info (the "i" circle) and tap Uninstall (or drag straight up to the Uninstall trash can).
  • Note: Do NOT tap "Remove" (that only deletes the shortcut).
STEP 2: Clear Old Site Storage in Chrome
  • Open Chrome and go to lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/
  • Tap the Tune / Sliders icon on the left side of the address bar.
  • Tap Cookies and site data (or Site settings) → tap the Trash Can / Clear & reset.
STEP 3: Refresh & Reinstall
  • Tap Chrome's three dots (top right) and tap the Refresh arrow.
  • Tap INSTALL in the top bar (or tap Chrome's three dots → Install app).
