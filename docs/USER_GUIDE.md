# Y.D.R.B.N. — Operator's Manual & Technical Data Sheet (v1.2)

**Your Daily Random Band Name (Y.D.R.B.N.)** is a 100% client-side, offline-capable 1970s stereo receiver, procedural vinyl sleeve compositor, and multi-instrument Web Audio API synthesizer Progressive Web App (PWA).

* **Live Turntable Deck:** [https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/](https://lharles.github.io/ydrbn-app/)
* **Aesthetic Inspiration:** Inspired by [*“Random Band Names, Volume One”*](https://www.google.com/search?q=Random+Band+Names+Charles+Key) by Charles Key.

---

## 1. Top Receiver Faceplate Controls

* **Archive (`Crate Icon`):** Opens your 14-slot **Vinyl Crate Archive**, where you can search pressings, filter Favorites, trigger **Auto-Spin College Radio**, or tap **`あ/A`** on any algorithmically generated drop to **Copy & Translate** it into any of the 16 supported languages.
* **Install (`Download Icon`):** Mounts Y.D.R.B.N. to your phone's home screen or desktop for 100% standalone offline operation.
* **Today:** Jumps straight to the newest pressing in your crate.
* **Favorite (`Heart Icon`):** Protects a pressing from rotating out when your 14-slot crate is full.
* **Share (`Share Icon`):** Exports high-resolution front/back sleeve JPEGs, formatted tracklists/bios, or a **1-Tap Pressing DNA Clone Link (`?dna=...`)**.
* **Apparel (`Shirt Icon`):** Opens the **300 DPI Apparel Fabrication Terminal** for exporting 3600×3600 chest cutouts/plaques and 3600×4800 tour-back schedules.

---

## 2. Main Turntable & Sleeve Operations

* **⚡ Daily Drop:** Press once every 24 hours to synthesize a brand-new fictional band, 8-track program, critical reviews, recording lore, and 3-layer cover art.
* **Flip Sleeve / Front Cover:** Rotates the 3D vinyl sleeve 180° to inspect the numbered tracklist, dead-wax runout groove etchings, condition grade (`MINT` down to `FAIR` with 0.55 Hz radiator-warp pitch wow), and liner notes.
* **Spin Audio Clip & Interactive Tracklist:** Press **Spin Audio Clip** (or tap any individual track 1–8 on the back cover) to mix era-matched 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s stems with live stereo VU meters and **33⅓ / 45 / 16⅔ RPM** speed toggling.
* **Read ↗ (Archival Liner Notes):** Opens a dedicated high-contrast modal displaying the full critical reception and band biography.

---

## 3. Customize A Drop & Custom Drop Studio

* **1. Swap Current Cover Art (On-Device Darkroom):** Stage any photo from your device and click **Darkroom Filter** to cycle non-repeating **Kodachrome, Cyanotype, Noir, and X-Ray** treatments across **Classic Crop, Cinematic Blur, and Triptych Split** layouts.
* **2. Create New Custom Drop:** Mint a standalone record anytime (without consuming your 24h Daily Drop timer), paste a `YDRBN-DNA:...` string to clone a friend's pressing, or check **Add new words & artwork into generator rotation** to harvest novel vocabulary into your local IndexedDB crate.

---

## 4. The Cargo Bay, Crate Forge & 16-Language Engine

* **16 Supported Locales:** Switch languages anytime via the footer globe badge (`EN`, `ES`, `PT`, `FR`, `IT`, `DE`, `ID`, `JA`, `ZH`, `HI`, `BN`, `UR`, `AR`, `RU`, `TLH` Klingon, and `PIG` Pig-Latin).
* **The Cargo Bay & Crate Forge:** Install modular word, subject cutout, and backdrop packs, or author and export custom `.json` crates directly on your device.
* **Director's Chair:** Isolate custom expansion packs by toggling default word, subject, backdrop, or emoji pools on or off.
